Privacy Policy
Plapperland – Ilos Wortreise

Last updated: 2026-09-29

In short: this app collects no data.

What the app does not do

  • No personal data is collected, stored or transmitted.
  • No ads, no sign-in, no account.
  • No tracking, no analytics or statistics services, no third-party SDKs.
  • The app works offline and makes no network connections of its own (apart from the purchase via Apple, see below).
  • The app does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts.

In-app purchase

  • The only exception to offline use is the optional one-time unlock of all worlds (in-app purchase). Purchase and “Restore purchases” are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we receive no payment or personal data.
  • The purchase is only reachable in the parent area behind a parental gate.

Data stored on the device

  • Game progress (for example collected stickers, music and voice settings, play-time setting) is stored only locally on the device and is never shared.
  • Deleting the app removes this data. Progress and stickers can also be reset in the parent area.

Children

The app is made for young children. Because it collects no personal data at all, no data about children is collected either. Parent settings and the purchase are protected by a parental gate.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) you have rights of access, rectification, erasure and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As the app processes no personal data, there is normally nothing we hold about you; if you contact us by email, we use your message only to answer it.

Changes

If the app's handling of data ever changes, this page will be updated before the change is released.
